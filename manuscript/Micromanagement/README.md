# 3 Micromanagement

C> *To ensure that your whole host may withstand the brunt of the enemy’s attack and remain unshaken — this is effected by maneuvers direct and indirect.*
C>
C> -- Sun Tzu "Art of War"

Micromanagement (micro) refers to everything that covers a single main screen. Something is happening on it at any given moment. All of this relates to micro.

Let us imagine all the events on the map as a large puzzle. Then, events at the micro level are one small piece. Macromanagement (macro) considers the entire puzzle.

Every champion in League of Legends could perform the following basic actions:

1. Attacking
2. Using abilities
3. Movement.

All of these actions are related to micromanagement.

In this chapter, we will look at how to execute basic actions effectively. There are several fundamental techniques for this. They allow you to maximize your champion's potential: dealing more damage and surviving longer in teamfights. This gives you an advantage over your opponents simply through better execution.

Mastering any micromanagement technique requires regular practice and repetition. We will discuss how to do it correctly.
